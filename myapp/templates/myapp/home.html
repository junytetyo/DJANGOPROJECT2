My Teaching Values

The principles I want to carry into every learning space.

Every learner belongs

Build a respectful classroom where students feel safe to participate and be themselves.

Curiosity leads to discovery

Encourage questions, exploration, and active learning—not just memorizing answers.

Make learning accessible

Use flexible approaches and resources so every student has a way to engage and grow.

Keep learning together

Reflect, listen to feedback, and keep improving as a learner and future educator.
